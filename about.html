About

Who is writing this, and why.

Who I am

I build automation systems and intelligent workflows for businesses that can't afford enterprise software but need enterprise capability.

Primary stack: Google Workspace, Apps Script, Claude API. I work with small businesses. Based everywhere. Building in Texas.

My interests run wide — economics, AI, insurance, startups, leadership, whatever I'm reading this week. I have opinions on most of it.

Occasionally something in public discourse gets so thoroughly mangled by motivated analysis that I feel compelled to write about it.

This site is where that writing lives.

How this works

Every piece starts with a question I can't find an honest answer to anywhere else.

I bring that question to Claude (Anthropic's AI). We pull primary data — RBI bulletins, MoSPI press notes, World Bank datasets, parliamentary records, company filings.

We build the analysis together. I challenge the outputs. Claude challenges my assumptions. We go back and forth until the answer is honest.

The result is what you read here.

No political affiliation. No institutional position to protect. No advertiser to please. No agenda except the question.

Claude has no vote, no party, no editor telling it what conclusions to reach. That's why this works.

Disclosure: This site uses Claude (Anthropic) as a research and writing collaborator. All data is from primary sources — RBI, MoSPI, World Bank, parliamentary records. All editorial positions are those of HR Srinivas. Claude's outputs are reviewed, challenged, and edited before publication. This is AI-assisted writing, not AI-generated content.
